# larpr feature launch: brag plan

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, **30.0s**. That's over brag's usual 15–25s because the brief asks for most of the app's features, and nine features need about 2.5s each to be readable. **Tone:** `app-store` (clean feature cards, smooth slides) with the brand's playful hook.
**Tagline:** "Fake it till you make it. We'll track the making." The first half lands in the hook and the full line closes the video. The "Strava for skills" line is not used anywhere on screen.

## Rubric
- **What is it?** larpr: log practice in any hobby, prove it, and grow from Larping to Legit with people learning the same thing.
- **Who is it for?** Hobbyists who say they "play piano" and want it to become true.
- **What sets it apart?** Proof (Larp Check, video), visible progress (skill trees, tiers), and a community built around one craft: feed and kudos, mentors, side quests, leaderboard, guilds and live rooms.
- **Funniest claim:** "I play piano." Stamped LARPING.
- **Visual hook:** the claim gets stamped, then "Fake it till you make it." arrives.
- **Real UI and data:** the Piano skill (`#2F8FDE`, "Great timing" kudos, class Bard) and its real tree from `seed.sql` (Technique: C major scale, All major scales, Arpeggios · Reading: Treble clef, Both clefs, Sight-reading · Chords: Triads, Inversions, Improvisation). Also the real quest "Learn a song in 7 days", the guild "Adult Beginners Piano Night", the room "Quiet scales room", the Arena tabs, "Ranked by verified practice minutes.", the mentorship copy, the share-card footer "larpr · get good at things, together", and the focus timer, Larp Check, PostCard and tier pills.
- **Share caption:** "Fake it till you make it. We'll track the making."

**Privacy:** Sam, the mentor Kai and the leaderboard names are fictional stand-ins; no seed users or database rows appear. The mentor reply is illustrative UI text.

## Story device
One phone stays on screen while its screen slides between features. The left column carries a **Day N** counter (1 → 240) and a feature rail fills dot by dot, so the whole tour reads as one learner's arc.

## Music
120 BPM, A minor. Kick and hats from frame 0, then bass and clap from 4.0 and plucks from 9.0. Each feature cut gets a soft swell, plus a small in-key cue for that feature: tick, node chimes, Larp Check blip, kudos sparkle, message pop, quest check, rank-up, RSVP tap, tier chimes. The final hit is at 26.5.
